## 🔬 /research

### Language-Switching in DeepSeek-R1/V3 Reasoning

An independent 50-run controlled investigation into why the Chain-of-Thought (CoT) reasoning in DeepSeek-R1/V3 spontaneously switches to Chinese under specific English prompts.

> [!NOTE]
> **Core Finding:** DeepSeek's Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) router triggers Chinese-language reasoning experts when high technical jargon density co-occurs with numeric scoring formats—bypassing English system override instructions before user directives take effect.

| Metric / Milestone | Detail |
|:---|:---|
| **Methodology** | 50 controlled runs across 5 conditions (T1–T5) isolating technical density vs scoring schemes |
| **Technical Report** | [github.com/AbuAnsari-06/deepseek-cot-language-investigation →](https://github.com/AbuAnsari-06/deepseek-cot-language-investigation) |
| **Upstream Impact** | Documented and filed upstream bug report: [DeepSeek-R1 Issue #863 →](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1/issues/863) |

### 02 · CarbonMitra — Satellite-Driven dMRV & Carbon Credit Marketplace

A decentralized Measurement, Reporting, and Verification (dMRV) platform empowering smallholder farmers to tokenize verified soil carbon sequestration into ERC-20 carbon credits on Polygon Amoy.

- **Satellite Remote Sensing:** Automated NDVI calculation directly from Sentinel-2 L2A multispectral bands (B04 Red, B08 NIR).
- **Automated Verification:** Transparent soil carbon verification pipeline removing corporate greenwashing in carbon finance.
- **Stack:** `React` · `TypeScript` · `Leaflet GIS` · `Sentinel-2 L2A API` · `Polygon Amoy`
- **Repository:** [github.com/AbuAnsari-06/CarbonMitra →](https://github.com/AbuAnsari-06/CarbonMitra)

---

### 03 · NarrateAI — Multi-Voice Audiobook Synthesis Engine

An audio performance engine that transforms story prose into an expressive multi-character performance with emotion-driven speech modulation.

- **Speech Dynamics:** Parses raw text into character emotion profiles and maps them to speech pitch, rate, and volume via the Web Speech API.
- **Acoustic Separation:** Gender-banded pitch separation ensuring audible distinction between narrative voices.
- **Stack:** `React` · `JavaScript` · `Web Speech API` · `Client Audio Engine`
- **Repository:** [github.com/AbuAnsari-06/NarrateAI →](https://github.com/AbuAnsari-06/NarrateAI)
