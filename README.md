## /research

### Language-switching in DeepSeek-R1/V3 reasoning

A 50-run controlled investigation into why the Chain-of-Thought of DeepSeek-R1/V3 sometimes switches to Chinese under specific English prompts.

- Wrote a full technical report and published the repo: [github.com/AbuAnsari-06/deepseek-cot-language-investigation](https://github.com/AbuAnsari-06/deepseek-cot-language-investigation)
- Filed the finding upstream as [issue #863 on deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1/issues/863)
- Shared the results on Reddit, LinkedIn and email

<summary><b>UNISAFETY</b></summary>

<br/>

Real-time crowd-verified public safety and emergency coordination platform. Connects community members through GPS proximity consensus, flash emergency SOS alerts, hyper-local area chats, and situation intelligence summaries.

- **Stack:** React, TypeScript, Vite, Leaflet GIS, Firebase (Firestore, Auth)
- **Key features:** Geotagged hazard reporting, interactive marker clustering, real-time SOS broadcast, and automated situation summaries

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>CarbonMitra</b></summary>

<br/>

Satellite-driven direct measurement, reporting, and verification (dMRV) and carbon credit marketplace for smallholder agriculture. Verifies soil carbon sequestration from Sentinel-2 satellite imagery and issues ERC-20 carbon credits on Polygon Amoy.

- **Stack:** React, TypeScript, Leaflet GIS, Sentinel-2 L2A API, Polygon Amoy
- **Key features:** Automated NDVI vegetation index calculation from multispectral satellite bands, transparent soil carbon verification, and ERC-20 token issuance
- **Link:** [github.com/AbuAnsari-06/CarbonMitra](https://github.com/AbuAnsari-06/CarbonMitra)

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>NarrateAI</b></summary>

<br/>

Turns a story into a voiced performance. Parses text into structured character and emotion profiles, then uses the Web Speech API to give each character a distinct voice with emotion-mapped pitch, rate, and volume.

- **Stack:** React, JavaScript, Web Speech API
- **Key features:** Dynamic voice-assignment engine, gender-banded pitch separation, and guaranteed audible distinctness between characters
- **Link:** [github.com/AbuAnsari-06/NarrateAI](https://github.com/AbuAnsari-06/NarrateAI)

</details>
